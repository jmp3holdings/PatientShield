How We Protect Your Information

Hospital bills and medical records are among the most private documents a person has. Here is how we handle yours — in plain language, including the limits.

Our practices

  • Encryption in transit. Your uploads travel over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections, and our site requires HTTPS everywhere.
  • Access is limited. Only the people working on your case can open your documents. Our administrative system requires a login and is not public.
  • No sale, no ads, no public training. We never sell or rent your information, we do not use it for advertising, and your documents are not used to train public AI models.
  • Retention limits. We keep your documents while your case is open. When a case closes, its documents are deleted; the record of your consent is kept as proof of the authorization you gave us.
  • Your authorization controls sharing. We share your documents with a hospital only under the written authorization in your service agreement.

The limits, plainly

  • No internet system can be guaranteed 100% secure. We use reputable infrastructure providers and limit access, but risk can be reduced, not eliminated.
  • Email is not always a secure channel. Where a document is especially sensitive, ask us about the secure portal link we provide for your case instead of emailing attachments.
  • If a security incident affects your information, we will notify you promptly and as required by applicable breach-notification laws.

Questions

Contact admin@argubill.com with any question about how your documents are handled, or to request access to or deletion of your information.
