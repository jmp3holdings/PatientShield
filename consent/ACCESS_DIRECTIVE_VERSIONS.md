# ArguBill — Right-of-Access Directive Text Versions (archive)

Every intake consent record stores the version and the full text the customer agreed to. This file archives the exact wording per version so any stored record can be matched to its text. New versions are added here in the same change that ships them; archived versions are never edited.

## 2026-11-12-v1 (in force from Nov 12, 2026)
"Right-of-Access Directive (required). I direct the hospital or facility I name in this form to send copies of my records relating to my hospital billing directly to ArguBill, my designated third party, under my right of access to my own health information. Records should be sent to ArguBill at admin@argubill.com or through ArguBill's secure intake. By checking this box and submitting this form, I sign this directive electronically, and the date and time of my acceptance are recorded."

What this instrument is: a patient right-of-access directive under 45 CFR 164.524(c)(3)(ii) directing the provider to send records to ArguBill as the designated third party, flagged for counsel confirmation.
