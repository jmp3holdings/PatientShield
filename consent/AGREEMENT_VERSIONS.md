# ArguBill — Consent / Agreement Text Versions (archive)

Every intake consent record stores the version and the full text the customer agreed to. This file archives the exact wording per version so any stored record can be matched to its text. New versions are added here in the same change that ships them; archived versions are never edited.

## 2026-11-03-v1 (in force Nov 3 – Nov 12, 2026)
"Service agreement & authorization (required). I agree to the ArguBill service terms: the bill review is free, there is no upfront cost, and I pay only if my hospital balance is lowered — 25% of the verified savings, never more than $1,000. If my balance is not lowered, I pay $0. I authorize ArguBill to contact the hospital's billing department about my bill, to share the documents I provide, and to receive billing information on my behalf."

## 2026-11-12-v2 (in force from Nov 12, 2026)
"Service agreement & authorization (required). I agree to the ArguBill service terms: the bill review is free, there is no upfront cost, and I pay only if my hospital balance is lowered — 25% of the verified savings, never more than $1,000. If my balance is not lowered, I pay $0. I authorize ArguBill to receive, store, and use, for my case, my hospital bills, explanations of benefits (EOBs), medical records, and any other forms or documents relating to my hospital billing. I authorize the hospital or facility I name in this form, and its billing department, to disclose those records and my billing information to ArguBill, so ArguBill can review my bill, identify errors, and dispute charges on my behalf. This authorization expires when my case is closed. I may revoke it in writing at any time; revoking stops future disclosures but does not undo disclosures already made in reliance on it. I understand that information disclosed under this authorization may be shared as needed for my case and may then no longer be protected by HIPAA privacy rules. By checking this box and submitting this form, I sign this authorization electronically, and the date and time of my acceptance are recorded. I understand that my information travels over encrypted connections, is kept in access-controlled storage limited to the people working on my case, and is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models."

What changed in v2: the authorization now expressly covers receiving, storing, and using all document types a customer may give ArguBill (hospital bills, EOBs, medical records, and other forms relating to the billing case); it is drafted to track the HIPAA authorization core elements (information covered, who may disclose, who receives, purpose, expiration at case closure, written-revocation right, redisclosure notice, electronic signature with recorded date/time) and is flagged for counsel confirmation; and a data-handling acknowledgment was added (encrypted connections; access-controlled storage limited to the people working on the case; never sold; never used for advertising; never used to train AI models).

## 2026-11-12-v3 (in force from Nov 12, 2026)
"Service agreement & authorization (required). I agree to the ArguBill service terms: the bill review is free, there is no upfront cost, and I pay only if my hospital balance is lowered — 25% of the verified savings, never more than $1,000. If my balance is not lowered, I pay $0. You authorize ArguBill to work on your case using the documents you provide. I understand that my information travels over encrypted connections, is kept in access-controlled storage limited to the people working on my case, and is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models. Arbitration; waiver of jury trial and class actions. Any dispute arising out of or relating to these terms or to ArguBill’s services will be resolved by binding arbitration on an individual basis instead of in court. You and ArguBill each waive the right to a trial by jury and the right to bring or participate in a class action or other representative proceeding. This paragraph does not apply to claims that cannot legally be arbitrated or waived, and nothing in these terms limits ArguBill’s liability for its own negligence or willful misconduct. Liability limit; scope of service. ArguBill’s total liability for any claim relating to its services is limited to the fees you actually paid ArguBill for your case (which is $0 if your balance was not lowered). ArguBill provides a bill review and advocacy service based on the documents you provide; we do not guarantee that any bill will be reduced or that any specific result will be achieved. By checking this box and submitting this form, I sign this agreement and authorization electronically, and the date and time of my acceptance are recorded."

What changed in v3: authorization content moved out into the standalone HIPAA Authorization + Right-of-Access Directive (compound-authorization rule); dispute/liability/scope sections remain in the agreement.
