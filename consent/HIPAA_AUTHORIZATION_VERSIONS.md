# ArguBill — HIPAA Authorization Text Versions (archive)

Every intake consent record stores the version and the full text the customer agreed to. This file archives the exact wording per version so any stored record can be matched to its text. New versions are added here in the same change that ships them; archived versions are never edited.

## 2026-11-12-v1 (in force from Nov 12, 2026)
"HIPAA Authorization (required). I authorize the hospital or facility I name in this form, and its billing department, to disclose my protected health information relating to my hospital billing — including my hospital bills and itemized statements, explanations of benefits (EOBs), and medical records relating to the billed services — to ArguBill. The purpose of this disclosure is for ArguBill to review my hospital bill, identify billing errors, and dispute charges on my behalf. This authorization expires when my case is closed. I understand I may revoke this authorization in writing at any time; revoking it stops future disclosures but does not undo disclosures already made in reliance on it. I understand that signing this authorization is voluntary and that my treatment, payment, enrollment, or eligibility for benefits will not be conditioned on whether I sign it. I understand that information disclosed under this authorization may be redisclosed by the recipient as needed for my case and may then no longer be protected by HIPAA privacy rules. This authorization does not cover psychotherapy notes or substance-use-disorder treatment records. By checking this box and submitting this form, I sign this authorization electronically, and the date and time of my acceptance are recorded."

What this instrument is: standalone authorization kept separate from the engagement agreement under the compound-authorization rule (45 CFR 164.508(b)(3)), carrying the six core elements + three required statements, excluding psychotherapy notes and Part 2 substance-use records, flagged for counsel confirmation.
